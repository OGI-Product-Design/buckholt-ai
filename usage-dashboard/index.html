Mobius PAS usage

Period 2 covers 10 August to 8 September 2026, read against Period 1, 11 July to 9 August 2026. Both periods were built the same way: fresh queries only, no dashboard widgets, and sessions rather than raw event fires. Jaunt is excluded throughout — it is API-driven and not comparable to interface usage.

Every workflow figure on this page measures intent to act, not the outcome of acting
A broker clicking “Cancel Policy” is counted. A policy actually being cancelled is not, because no completion event exists for any of these workflows — not for quote-to-policy conversion, payment, renewal, MTA or cancellation. Two rows are page views; everything else is a click on a button or a link. Read every number below as a count of sessions that reached toward a workflow.

Asked for, and deliberately not shown

Four metrics were sought and are absent. An absent metric is a finding, so each one is stated here rather than dropped quietly out of a table.

Crash rate — no result either period No result came back in Period 1 or Period 2.
Network error rate — excluded, not comparable Period 2 returned 0.05%, but that reflects one specific known issue across five sessions, not a general error rate. Period 1 has no figure at all. Showing them together would imply a trend that does not exist.
Average API response — no buildable mean No mean can be built in either period. Only median and 95th percentile per URL are possible, and that has not been built.
Convert quote → policy — no event exists No bind or success event exists anywhere in the data. See the flags below.

Flags

6 open

Each flag carries the confidence the evidence actually supports. Three are confirmed, two are not, and one is a question about the instrumentation rather than about brokers.

Payment-plan account page returns HTTP 500

Confirmed · high

The policy-details endpoint fails on the payment-plan account page. Brokers see it as an error modal that forces them to navigate away. Seen again 13 August – 7 September, still untriaged.

Evidence
Confirmed by replay in two prior periods plus this period’s error data — three independent checks.
Next
The strongest-evidenced issue in the report. Ready to raise with engineering.

The money path cannot be measured at all

Unresolved

No bind event and no payment-confirmation event exist anywhere in the data, so quote-to-policy conversion and payment success are both unmeasurable. Everything on this page stops at the click.

Evidence
Unresolved in both periods. This is the single biggest gap in the report.
Next
It will not close without new instrumentation. Two custom events would make it answerable in the next edition.

Cancel Policy is clicked ~5× as often as Renewal

Confirmed · 2 periods

Second period running at essentially the same ratio — 5.1× then 5.0× — with both intents growing at a similar rate.

Next
Confirm directly what the Cancel Policy control does on the screens where it is clicked. Real cancellation volume at five times renewal volume would be a striking business fact; a mislabelled navigation click would not.

Documents dominate the working session

Repeats from P1

Attachments is touched in two Islands sessions in five — roughly five times Retrieve Quote volume, and consistent with Period 1.

Next
Worth checking whether the attachment list is searchable, or whether brokers are scrolling to find files.

Pizza fell sharply, on a very small base

Low confidence

Sessions down 37.9%, quote-starts down from seven to four, and page load at 2,731ms against Islands’ 1,340ms.

Evidence
Three readings fit equally well: genuine declining adoption, PZ01/PZ91 identification missing users, or performance driving people away.
Next
Not enough volume to tell which. Confirm identification before drawing any adoption conclusion.

Client Homepage Viewed may be firing in a loop

Open question

The raw event fired about 155 times per session in Period 1, against 23 times per session on Record Summary Viewed. That points at a re-render or route-change loop rather than 155 genuine visits.

Evidence
The raw fire count was not re-checked in Period 2, so this is unconfirmed either way. Session counts are used throughout this dashboard and are safe regardless.
Next
Re-check the fire rate. The raw count — 334,785 in Period 1 — should not be used in any reporting.

Data notes

Everything that could change how a figure on this page should be read.

  • Fresh queries only. No dashboard widgets. Sessions, not raw event fires.
  • Jaunt is excluded throughout by construction — it is API-driven and not comparable to interface usage.
  • Segment filter logic: Islands = User ID contains IS91 or IS01; Pizza = PZ01 or PZ91; Greenlight = BR01 or BR91.
  • Six to eight of Period 2’s intent metrics ran on a ~1-day-offset rolling window (≈9 Aug – 9 Sep) rather than the exact 10 Aug – 8 Sep dates. Unlikely to matter at 30-day scale; noted for completeness.
  • Pizza’s session-duration figure used a different filter (duration > 0s) from Islands and Greenlight (all sessions). Affects that one row only.
  • Islands segment ID confirmed as 1370959 throughout both periods. A second ID (1333928) appears unused and is worth retiring on the LogRocket side to avoid future ambiguity.
  • Active users, and duration and page load for Pizza and Greenlight, were not built in Period 1 — LogRocket cannot split those chart types by segment without a duplicate metric per brand.

Sources
