Key pages & actions

Nine pages and actions can be compared across both periods, and every one of them grew faster than Islands sessions did. That is the finding: not one metric moving, but nine independently measured ones moving the same way. Brokers who used the platform did more per session — the same people, working harder in the tool, rather than more people arriving.

Clicks and page visits do not confirm completion
These Islands figures count sessions containing a page view or click. They do not confirm that a quote, payment, renewal, MTA, cancellation, download or other workflow was completed.

Flags

6 open

Each flag carries the confidence the evidence actually supports. Three are confirmed, two are not, and one is a question about the instrumentation rather than about brokers.

Payment-plan account page returns HTTP 500

Confirmed · high

The policy-details endpoint fails on the payment-plan account page. Brokers see it as an error modal that forces them to navigate away. Seen again 13 August – 7 September, still untriaged.

Evidence
Confirmed by replay in two prior periods plus this period’s error data — three independent checks.
Next
The strongest-evidenced issue in the report. Ready to raise with engineering.

The money path cannot be measured at all

Unresolved

No bind event and no payment-confirmation event exist anywhere in the data, so quote-to-policy conversion and payment success are both unmeasurable. Everything on this page stops at the click.

Evidence
Unresolved in both periods. This is the single biggest gap in the report.
Next
It will not close without new instrumentation. Two custom events would make it answerable in the next edition.

Cancel Policy is clicked ~5× as often as Renewal

Confirmed · 2 periods

Second period running at essentially the same ratio — 5.1× then 5.0× — with both actions growing at a similar rate.

Next
Confirm directly what the Cancel Policy control does on the screens where it is clicked. Real cancellation volume at five times renewal volume would be a striking business fact; a mislabelled navigation click would not.

Documents dominate the working session

Repeats from Previous 30 days

Attachments is touched in two Islands sessions in five — roughly five times Retrieve Quote volume, and consistent with Previous 30 days.

Next
Worth checking whether the attachment list is searchable, or whether brokers are scrolling to find files.

Pizza fell sharply, on a very small base

Low confidence

Sessions down 37.9%, quote-starts down from seven to four, and page load at 2,731ms against Islands’ 1,340ms.

Evidence
Three readings fit equally well: genuine declining adoption, PZ01/PZ91 identification missing users, or performance driving people away.
Next
Not enough volume to tell which. Confirm identification before drawing any adoption conclusion.

Client Homepage Viewed may be firing in a loop

Open question

The raw event fired about 155 times per session in Previous 30 days, against 23 times per session on Record Summary Viewed. That points at a re-render or route-change loop rather than 155 genuine visits.

Evidence
The raw fire count was not re-checked in Last 30 days, so this is unconfirmed either way. Session counts are used throughout this dashboard and are safe regardless.
Next
Re-check the fire rate. The raw count — 334,785 in Previous 30 days — should not be used in any reporting.

Unavailable metrics

  • Crash rate: no result for Last 30 days or Previous 30 days.
  • Average API response: no buildable mean for either date range.
  • Network error rate as a trend: unavailable. Previous 30 days returned no result; Last 30 days returned 0.05% for one known issue (5 of 9,318 sessions), not a general error rate.
  • Quote → policy conversion: unavailable because no bind or success event exists.

Method & coverage

  • Fresh queries only; no dashboard widgets; sessions rather than raw event fires.
  • Jaunt is excluded because it is API-driven and not comparable to interface usage.
  • Segment filters: Islands = IS91 or IS01; Pizza = PZ01 or PZ91; Greenlight = BR01 or BR91.
  • Six to eight Last 30 days page/action metrics used an approximately one-day-offset window (≈9 Aug–9 Sep).
  • Pizza duration used duration > 0s; Islands and Greenlight used all sessions.
  • Active users, duration and page load for Pizza and Greenlight were not built for Previous 30 days.

Sources

Three supplied Mobius PAS usage reports: Previous 30 days (11 Jul–9 Aug, P1), Last 30 days (10 Aug–8 Sep, P2), and the combined edition. Figures are transcribed; weighted page load, shares and ratios are calculated in data.js.
