---
title: Legacy Figma libraries
lead: Products not yet on Buckholt need their own Figma library, so anchors and extended screens match how the product looks today.
---

## Who does it

A designer for each legacy product.

## What you need

- The product's existing Figma components
- The product pack, with its styling mode set to Legacy or Hybrid

## Steps

1. Publish the product's components as a Figma library.
2. Name it after the product, so {{agent:extend}} and {{agent:align}} pick the right one.
3. Record the library in the product pack.
4. Enable it in the product's design files.

## Done when

Designers can make anchor screens from the library, and {{agent:align}} reports no "not from the library" issues on a test frame.

## Used by

Designers making anchor screens, {{agent:extend}} and {{agent:align}}.
