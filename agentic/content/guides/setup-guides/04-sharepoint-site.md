title: SharePoint site
lead: SharePoint holds the discovery master copy and a copy of every product pack, so people outside Cursor can read them.
---

## Who does it

Laurence, with a SharePoint site owner.

## What you need

- A SharePoint site for the UX team, or rights to create one

## Steps

1. Create the site, or a section of the UX team's site.
2. Add three libraries: UX briefs, Discovery, and Product packs.
3. Give the UX team edit access and product managers read access.
4. Give Copilot access, so {{agent:brief}}, {{agent:insight}} and {{agent:map}} can read and save there.
5. Give {{agent:archive}} access, so it can copy packs in and pull approved discovery out.

## Done when

A UX brief saved in SharePoint can be linked on a design item, and {{agent:archive}} can copy a pack into the Product packs library.

## Used by

{{agent:brief}}, {{agent:insight}}, {{agent:map}} and {{agent:archive}}.
