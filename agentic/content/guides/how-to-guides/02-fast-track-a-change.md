title: Fast-track a change
lead: Go from Discover straight to Design when a change is small and well understood.
---

## When a change can be fast-tracked

{{agent:brief}} chooses the route and records why. A change can take the fast track only when all three are true:

- there is no new flow
- no new research is needed
- only existing screens change

If any one is false, the feature takes the full route.

## What is skipped

On the fast track, the feature goes from Discover straight to Design. Explore is skipped: {{agent:range}} runs only on full-route items, so there are no sandbox explorations, no {{agent:critic}} review and no shortlist gate.

## What is not skipped

- The UX brief, discovery and the design worklist.
- The design sign-off gate, with designers, the PM and an engineer.
- Prototype and handoff, including {{agent:audit}}'s checks.
- Handoff by {{agent:relay}}.

## Anchor screens on the fast track

The proposal: because only existing screens change, the current screens act as the anchors. Designers mark the frames being changed as anchors in Figma, update them where the change needs a new decision, and move the status to Anchors ready. {{agent:extend}} then works as usual.
