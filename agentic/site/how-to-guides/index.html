How-to guides

Short guides for the jobs that come up again and again.

Guides

  1. Run a feature end to end: from Ready for design to Handed over, on the full route.
  2. Fast-track a change: go from Discover straight to Design when a change is small and well understood.
  3. Add a non-Buckholt component: when Buckholt has no component for what the design needs, add it as a flagged, registered extension.
  4. Onboard a legacy product: bring a product that is not on Buckholt into the workflow without changing how it looks.
  5. Update a product pack: change a product pack in the repo, get it approved, and Archive updates the SharePoint copy.
