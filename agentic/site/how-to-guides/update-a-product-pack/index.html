Update a product pack

Change a product pack in the repo, get it approved, and Archive updates the SharePoint copy.

Steps

  1. Change the pack in /context on a branch and open a pull request.
  2. Laurence or Jon reviews and approves it. Every product pack change needs that approval.
  3. Merge the pull request.
  4. Archive updates the pack's SharePoint copy so the two match.

What Archive never does

  • Change a pack without approval.
  • Delete history.

Keeping packs current

In its weekly check, Archive flags packs older than 90 days. Every agent reads the product pack, so an out-of-date pack affects every feature for that product.
