Fast-track a change

Go from Discover straight to Design when a change is small and well understood.

When a change can be fast-tracked

Brief chooses the route and records why. A change can take the fast track only when all three are true:

  • there is no new flow
  • no new research is needed
  • only existing screens change

If any one is false, the feature takes the full route.

What is skipped

On the fast track, the feature goes from Discover straight to Design. Explore is skipped: Range runs only on full-route items, so there are no sandbox explorations, no Critic review and no shortlist gate.

What is not skipped

  • The UX brief, discovery and the design worklist.
  • The design sign-off gate, with designers, the PM and an engineer.
  • Prototype and handoff, including Audit 's checks.
  • Handoff by Relay .
