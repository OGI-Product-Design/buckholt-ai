Run a feature end to end

From Ready for design to Handed over, on the full route.

Before you start

The feature needs its fields complete: problem, outcome, products, users, priority, quarter and constraints. Each product it touches needs a product pack.

Discover

  1. Product sets the feature to Ready for design. Brief checks the fields, finds the product packs, chooses the route and writes the UX brief.
  2. The PM confirms scope. A designer moves the status to Discovery.
  3. Insight synthesises research and pulls LogRocket baselines. Designers validate the insights.
  4. Map maps the journeys and writes the screen inventory and design worklist. Designers approve, then move the status to Discovery approved.
  5. Archive writes the discovery summary into the repo.

Explore

  1. Range builds 5 to 10 greybox directions in the sandbox and posts an index.
  2. Critic stress-tests each direction and recommends up to three.
  3. Shortlist gate. Designers, the PM and an engineer agree direction, scope and early feasibility. Status: Shortlist agreed.

Design

  1. Designers make the anchor screens in Figma. The status moves to Anchors ready.
  2. Extend builds out the remaining screens in batches of up to five. Designers review every batch.
  3. Align checks each batch for consistency. Designers decide what to fix.
  4. Design sign-off gate. Designers, the PM and an engineer agree the design meets the success measures and is buildable. Status: Design signed off.

Prototype and test

  1. Build builds the prototype in the product's styling mode and posts the preview.
  2. Audit runs axe, keyboard, contrast and Buckholt checks. A critical accessibility issue blocks testing.
  3. Observe drafts the test plan. Designers run the sessions; Observe tags the recordings and writes findings.
  4. Findings go back to Extend or Build until designers mark testing complete.

Hand over

  1. Relay writes the specs and UX acceptance criteria and adds them to each engineering story.
  2. Relay sets Design status on the feature to Handed over.
  3. After release, Relay checks the build against the design and the success measures, and posts the report.
