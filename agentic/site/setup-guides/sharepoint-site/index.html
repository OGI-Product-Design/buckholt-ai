SharePoint site

SharePoint holds the discovery master copy and a copy of every product pack, so people outside Cursor can read them.

Guide 4 of 11

Who does it

Laurence or Jon, with a SharePoint site owner.

What you need

  • A SharePoint site for the UX team, or rights to create one

Steps

  1. Create the site, or a section of the UX team's site.
  2. Add three libraries: UX briefs, Discovery, and Product packs.
  3. Give the UX team edit access and product managers read access.
  4. Give Copilot access, so Brief , Insight and Map can read and save there.
  5. Give Archive access, so it can copy packs in and pull approved discovery out.

Done when

A UX brief saved in SharePoint can be linked on a design item, and Archive can copy a pack into the Product packs library.

Used by

Brief , Insight , Map and Archive .
