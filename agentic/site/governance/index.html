Governance and rollout

People own every decision. This page says who owns what, how work is reviewed and how we will know the workflow is working.

Reviews

Every agent stops at a human check. The main ones:

  • Designers validate Insight 's insights and approve Map 's flows and worklist.
  • Designers review every Extend batch.
  • Designers decide which of Align 's issues are mistakes and which are deliberate.
  • Designers check Build 's prototypes for fidelity and behaviour.
  • Audit blocks testing when it finds a critical accessibility issue.
  • Designers review before Relay hands over; engineering reviews feasibility.

Each agent's page lists its human gate.

Keeping the system healthy

Archive runs a weekly check and posts a health report. It flags:

  • product packs older than 90 days
  • extension register entries missing their Figma or code half
  • gates with no decision logged

It also tracks daily Figma MCP calls and warns the team at 80% of the limit.

After each gate, Archive adds the decision to the decision log.

Measures

Every feature has success measures.

  1. Brief drafts them in the UX brief.
  2. Insight pulls LogRocket baselines for them: completion, drop-off, errors and rage clicks on the flows in scope.
  3. Observe compares test results with the baselines.
  4. After release, Relay compares LogRocket data with the success measures and posts the report.

Rollout phases

The proposed rollout adds the agents in workflow order, one phase at a time, with a pilot feature first.

Phase
What is set up
Ready to move on when
1. Foundations Repo and locks, product packs, the design board, the SharePoint site. Archive starts. A pack exists for the pilot product and the board runs on stage statuses.
2. Discover Connectors for Copilot, Azure DevOps and Lucid. Brief , Insight and Map . One pilot feature reaches Discovery approved.
3. Explore and Design The sandbox, Figma MCP and Lucid MCP. Range , Critic , Extend and Align . The pilot passes the design sign-off gate.
4. Prototype and handoff /tests, LogRocket MCP and the Azure DevOps MCP. Build , Audit , Observe and Relay . The pilot is Handed over and the release check is posted.
5. All features Every new feature goes through the workflow. Reviewed after one planning increment.
