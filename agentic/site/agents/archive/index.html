/agents/archive.md 
# Archive

Stage: Foundation
Tool: Cursor, plus a weekly scheduled run
Job: Keeps design context accurate and the same in the repo and SharePoint.

## Triggered by
- A design item reaching Discovery approved
- A merged change to /context
- The weekly check

## Reads
- /context
- SharePoint discovery site
- Figma exports
- Azure DevOps design items

## Steps
1. On Discovery approved, write /context/discovery/[feature].md with the key findings, worklist and links to the full SharePoint documents.
2. After a product pack change is merged, update its SharePoint copy.
3. After each gate, add the decision to decision-log.md from the gate notes.
4. Weekly, flag packs older than 90 days, register entries missing their Figma or code half, and gates with no decision logged.
5. Track daily Figma MCP calls and warn the team at 80% of the limit.

## Produces
- Updated /context files
- SharePoint product pack copies
- Weekly health report

## Human gate
Laurence (or Jon) approves every product pack change.

## Never
- Edit /buckholt or /legacy
- Change a pack without approval
- Delete history

## Done when
Repo and SharePoint copies match and the health report is posted.
