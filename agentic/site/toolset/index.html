Toolset

Discovery happens in Microsoft 365. Exploring, designing and prototyping happen in Cursor. Archive keeps the two in step.

Two platforms and a bridge

Microsoft 365 side

  • Copilot runs Brief , Insight and Map .
  • SharePoint holds the discovery master copy and a copy of each product pack.
  • Teams holds recordings and transcripts.
  • Azure DevOps holds the design board, the stage status and the links between work items.
  • Lucid holds journey and process maps. It is also reached from Cursor.

Cursor side

  • Cursor runs the other nine agents.
  • Figma MCP: Extend writes; Align , Build and Relay only read.
  • LogRocket MCP is used by the Insight Cursor step, Observe and Relay .
  • The Buckholt prototyping repo holds Buckholt core, extensions, the sandbox and the context Archive maintains.
  • Playwright and axe run the automated checks for Audit .
  • Lucid MCP is used by Range for rough flows.

The bridge

Archive keeps the two sides in step. It copies product packs into SharePoint after a change is merged, and pulls approved discovery into the repo as /context/discovery/[feature].md.
